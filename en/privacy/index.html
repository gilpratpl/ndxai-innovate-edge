Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-14

This policy explains which personal data Neural Dynamics AI S.L. (NDXai) processes when you visit www.ndxai.eu or get in touch with us, for what purpose, and what rights you have. The data controller is the site owner.

What data we process and why

Contact. If you email us, call us or contact us on LinkedIn, we process the data you give us (name, company, email, phone and the content of your message) to reply and, where relevant, prepare a proposal.

Website chat. Messages you type to the website assistant are sent to our server and to a language model provider to generate the reply. Do not enter personal data or confidential information.

Analytics. If you accept it in the cookie notice, we use Google Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the site is used (pages visited, device type, approximate location). If you do not accept it, Google Analytics is not loaded.

Language preference. If you switch language, we store your choice in your browser (local storage) to remember it. This data does not leave your device.

Cookie preference. We store in your browser (local storage) whether you accepted or rejected analytics cookies, so we do not ask again for 12 months. This data does not leave your device.

Legal basis

We process contact and chat data to deal with your enquiry: taking steps at your request prior to entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in answering professional enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Analytics is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Art. 22(2) of the Spanish LSSI), which you can withdraw at any time from "Cookie settings" in the footer.

Cookies

The only cookies on the site are those set by Google Analytics (_ga and _ga_<identifier>), which distinguish visitors and sessions and expire after two years at most. They are only set if you accept them in the cookie notice.

You can change your choice at any time from "Cookie settings" in the footer. If you reject them after accepting, we delete them. You can also block or delete them in your browser settings.

Who we share data with

We do not sell or pass data to third parties. The providers that run the site are involved: GitHub (website hosting), Google (analytics and fonts) and Render (hosting of the chat server). Some of these providers are located in the United States.

How long we keep it

Contact data is kept for as long as the relationship lasts and, afterwards, for the time needed to meet possible legal liabilities. Analytics data is kept for the retention period configured in Google Analytics.

Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to the email address shown in the site owner details.

If you believe we have not handled your request properly, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and the last-updated date.
