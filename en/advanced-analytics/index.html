Advanced analytics and machine learning for data-driven decisions

We turn your company's data into a competitive advantage through Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning techniques. We create models that forecast sales, optimize inventory management, segment customers, or define dynamic pricing.

This enables better-informed decision-making and improves business efficiency. For example, a retailer can adjust promotions based on predicted demand, or a logistics company can optimize delivery routes.

Problems it solves

  • Sales or demand forecasts made by eye or in spreadsheets.
  • Too much stock of some products and stockouts of others.
  • Data spread across ERP, CRM and spreadsheets that nobody joins up.
  • Reports that explain what happened but not what will happen.

How we do it

  1. We start from a specific decision you want to improve, not from the data available.
  2. We bring together and clean the data from the different sources.
  3. We train forecasting, classification or optimisation models and compare them with the current method.
  4. We deliver the result where the decision is made: a dashboard, a regular report or directly in the management system.
  5. We track the model's accuracy over time and retrain it when the context changes.

Example applications

  • Demand forecasting to plan production and purchasing.
  • Inventory and reorder point optimisation.
  • Customer segmentation and churn risk detection.
  • Delivery route and vehicle load optimisation.

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from a BI dashboard?

BI shows what happened. Advanced analytics uses models to predict what will happen and recommend what to do.

Is my data good enough?

It is almost never perfect, and that does not stop you getting started. During the assessment we see what can be done with what exists and what is worth improving.

Which tools do you work with?

We choose the tools based on the case and the systems the client already has, and integrate the result with their ERP, CRM or database.
