Custom AI agents and bots for business

We develop AI bots and agents tailored to different needs: website or app integration, agents for internal information queries, or customer support.

These systems automate repetitive tasks, provide instant responses, and enhance user experience. For example, a customer service bot can resolve common inquiries about orders or billing, while an internal agent can help teams find documents or data instantly.

Problems it solves

  • Repetitive customer queries that tie up the team.
  • Internal information scattered across documents, emails and systems that are hard to search.
  • Admin tasks that involve copying data from one system to another.
  • Out-of-hours or multilingual support the team cannot cover.

How we do it

  1. We define what the agent should do, with which sources, and how far it can go without a person stepping in.
  2. We connect it to your documentation and systems (ERP, CRM, databases) so it answers with your own information, not generic content.
  3. We set limits: what it can read, which actions it can take and when it must hand over to a person.
  4. We test it with real cases from your team before opening it to customers.
  5. We review conversations to improve the answers and extend what it can do.

Example applications

  • A customer service bot on your website for questions about orders, lead times or invoicing.
  • An internal agent that finds procedures, datasheets or documents in seconds.
  • An agent that reads emails or orders, extracts the data and enters it into your system.
  • An agent for maintenance technicians with access to manuals and the incident history.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a bot and an AI agent?

A bot answers questions. An agent can also take actions: query a system, fill in a form or open a ticket, within the limits it is given.

Is our data protected?

The agent only accesses the sources it is authorised to use and is designed with security in mind from the start. The proposal sets out where the data is processed.

Can it be integrated into our website or tools?

Yes. It can be embedded in a website, an app or internal tools, and connected to existing systems through their APIs.
