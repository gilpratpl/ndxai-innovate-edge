Intelligent process automation with AI

We implement AI systems that automate repetitive and administrative processes, reducing errors and freeing up valuable time for your team.

This allows staff to focus on higher-value strategic tasks such as innovation or customer relations. For instance, automating invoice management or order processing can drastically reduce response times and increase productivity.

Problems it solves

  • Hours spent keying in data from invoices, delivery notes or orders.
  • Transcription errors that are caught late.
  • Processes that depend on the one person who knows how they are done.
  • Long response times because work piles up in queues.

How we do it

  1. We map the process with the people who run it every day and pick the steps worth automating.
  2. We use AI to read documents and emails, extract the data and classify them.
  3. We connect the output to the ERP or management system so nothing has to be typed twice.
  4. We keep a person in the loop to validate borderline cases.
  5. We measure time and errors before and after to confirm the improvement.

Example applications

  • Supplier invoice handling: reading, validation and posting.
  • Entry of orders received by email or as PDFs.
  • Classification and routing of emails and requests.
  • Regular reports generated from several sources.

Frequently asked questions

How is it different from traditional RPA?

RPA repeats clicks on fixed screens and breaks when the format changes. AI understands variable documents and text, so it covers cases RPA cannot.

Does it replace people on the team?

The aim is to take repetitive work off them so they can focus on higher-value tasks. Borderline cases are still validated by a person.

Where do we start?

With one well-defined process that has volume and clear rules. Once it works, it is extended to others.
